Privacy notice

Last updated: 6 October 2026.

Who runs Ravenous?

I am Malcolm Spina, the developer and person responsible for this free restaurant-discovery portfolio project. For privacy questions, requests or complaints, email malpoke1@gmail.com.

What information is used?

  • Location suggestions: Google receives the location text you type, after a short typing delay, to suggest places.
  • Restaurant searches: Google receives the restaurant or cuisine query and location when you submit a search. Restaurant photos also load from Google services.
  • Optional precise location: choosing “Use my current location” shares your coordinates with Google to identify your area and bias searches towards nearby restaurants, after browser permission.
  • Website delivery: Netlify receives network information, including your IP address, to deliver the site and operate and protect its hosting service. Google's services load when needed for typed location suggestions, a submitted search or chosen precise-location lookup, and receive network information such as your IP address and browser details.
  • Correspondence: if you email me, I use your contact details and message to respond or handle your request.

There are no visitor accounts, bookings, payments or saved-search database. Please enter only information needed for your restaurant search; a town or area is enough for manual location entry.

Why this information is used

I rely on legitimate interests in providing restaurant searches and suggestions, delivering and protecting this small service, and responding to routine enquiries. These interests are balanced against visitors' privacy through limited data use, clear explanations and no saved search history.

Optional precise-location sharing relies on your consent. You can decline and type a location instead. Select “Stop using precise location”, or edit the location, to clear coordinates and stop using them in later searches. This also cancels pending location updates; it cannot undo information already sent to Google. Processing necessary to handle statutory privacy requests and complaints relies on applicable legal obligations.

Providers

Google Maps Platform supplies suggestions, searches, geocoding and photos. Google processes information under its Privacy Policy, which is incorporated into this notice, and the Maps controller–controller terms. Google has its own responsibilities for that processing.

Netlify hosts the site; its Privacy Statement and Data Processing Agreement describe its responsibilities, including processing on my behalf. Email is handled through Google's Gmail service. I do not sell your searches or location.

How long information is kept

  • In Ravenous: queries, results and coordinates stay in the current page's memory. Reloading or leaving discards that state; Stop/manual editing also clears coordinates.
  • Email: routine enquiries are deleted within six months after closure, or sooner when no longer needed. Minimal complaint and rights-request records are reviewed for deletion twelve months after closure. Necessary records for an active investigation or legal claim are kept while that need continues, with monthly review.
  • Providers: clearing Ravenous does not delete provider records. Google's Maps documentation describes retaining logs while needed for operations, support, security and service improvement. Netlify controls its infrastructure-log retention; its published terms do not give a fixed period for this site's logs. I do not export or maintain a separate visitor-log archive. Provider retention and deletion arrangements are described in the policies and terms linked above.

Cookies and browser storage

Ravenous does not store search history in cookies or browser storage and has no advertising or optional analytics. Tested search features worked with third-party cookies allowed and blocked; no persistent application storage or cookies on the captured Google requests were observed. Your browser may cache website files and photos. While the site is private for testing, Netlify uses a login cookie to restrict access; owner tools may save display preferences. These testing features are separate from restaurant discovery.

International processing

Google and Netlify use international infrastructure, including in the United States. Their published safeguards include the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for covered certified US recipients and contractual safeguards where applicable. See Google's transfer information and Netlify's transfer terms for details and routes to obtain copies of safeguards. You can also ask me for further information.

Your choices, rights and complaints

Depending on the circumstances, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw precise-location consent as described above. Email malpoke1@gmail.com; please avoid unnecessary sensitive information.

I will acknowledge privacy complaints within 30 days, investigate, keep you informed where appropriate and explain the outcome. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

I will update this notice if the service or its processing changes.
