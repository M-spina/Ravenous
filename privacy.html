Information used and why

  • Google receives the location text you type for autocomplete, after a short typing delay.
  • When you submit a search, Google receives the restaurant or cuisine query and location text to retrieve restaurant results.
  • If you choose “Use my current location”, your browser requests location permission and Google receives your coordinates to identify your area. Coordinates may also bias subsequent restaurant searches towards nearby places.
  • Google's services load when the search page opens, receiving requests including network information such as your IP address and browser details. Viewing restaurant photos also makes requests to Google services.
  • Our hosting provider [HOST NAME] receives requests for the site and may keep access or security logs containing online identifiers. [CONFIRMED LOG TYPES AND PURPOSES].
  • If you contact us, we use the details and content of your message to respond and handle your request or complaint.

Ravenous does not offer visitor accounts or a database of saved searches or favourites. Searches, coordinates and restaurant results are held in the current page's application memory. Avoid entering information you do not need for a restaurant search.

Stopping precise-location use

Choose “Stop using precise location”, or edit the location manually, to discard stored coordinates and prevent them being used for later searches. The readable location remains available for manual searches. A pending location response cannot restore discarded coordinates. Withdrawing does not undo processing that already occurred, including a request already sent to Google.
